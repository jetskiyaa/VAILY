Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 17, 2026

VAILY is an unofficial fan project for viewing VALORANT-related store information.

Information We Collect

We may collect limited technical information such as browser type, device details, approximate region, and server logs required to operate the site. If you use the browser login flow, the site may temporarily process Riot redirect URL data or related authentication data that you submit.

How We Use Information

  • Provide the website’s features
  • Fetch and display requested store data
  • Maintain security and troubleshoot errors
  • Improve performance and usability

Temporary Authentication Data

If you use browser login, any Riot redirect URL or related tokens are used only to process your request. VAILY does not sell this data or use it for advertising. Any temporary handling or short-term logging should be limited to what is reasonably necessary to operate the service.

Cookies and Local Storage

VAILY may use basic browser storage or functional cookies for temporary site behavior, preferences, or session flow. The site does not currently present itself as a behavioral advertising platform.

Third-Party Services

This site may request data from third-party services such as Riot-related endpoints or Valorant metadata providers. Those services may have their own privacy terms.

Data Retention

We aim to keep personal or authentication-related data only as long as necessary to operate the site, resolve issues, or comply with legal obligations.

Your Choices

You may stop using the site at any time. Avoid sharing sensitive account information except where required for site functionality.
